# US equity feed

Twelve Data Business is the lowest-integration option for Pivluma because the desktop already supports its daily REST history and streaming quotes. Its US documentation describes coverage of listed Nasdaq and NYSE equities. The default real-time feed represents about 5% of US trading activity. That figure describes trade activity rather than the share of supported tickers. Confirm the plan and contractual rights for external desktop display, redistribution, historical caching, and derived forecasts before configuring a shared credential. [Business plans](https://twelvedata.com/pricing-business) · [US equities coverage](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/9935903-us-equities-market-data)

Massive is an alternative when a consolidated US feed is required. Its base business offering combines a real-time fair-market estimate with end-of-day consolidated data. Confirm the add-ons for executable trades or official quotes. Alpaca distinguishes IEX coverage from all-exchange SIP plans. Confirm end-user display rights separately for either option. [Massive Business](https://massive.com/business-stocks) · [Massive real-time quote FAQ](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/does-the-business-plan-include-real-time-trades-and-quotes-or-only-fair-market-value) · [Alpaca market data](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api)

The US stock reference endpoint was queried successfully for META and NFLX. The Stocks and ETFs category filters use the corresponding Twelve Data reference lists for symbol completion. Reference data lists instruments. It does not grant live price entitlements. [Reference data guide](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5620513-how-to-find-all-available-symbols-at-twelve-data)

Before activating a shared live service, verify daily history and live quotes for the default equity and ETF lists using a credential held server-side, with no key distributed in Windows packages. Then verify provider timestamps, coverage during market hours, rate limits, and fallback behavior. [Application market-data configuration](../README.md#connect-live-market-data)
